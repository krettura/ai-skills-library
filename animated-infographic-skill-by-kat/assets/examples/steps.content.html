How to write a hook
that earns the “…more”

01
Pick your pull
Make them trust you, feel something, or want something.
ProofEmotionBenefit
02
Open with it
Lead with one specific line a stranger would care about.
“I spent 4 months building something nobody wanted.”
03
Tease the rest
Say, outright or implied, what’s waiting below the fold.
“Here’s how I did it.” or “The answer surprised me.”
04
Check the cutoff
Preview where “…more” cuts you off on mobile and desktop.
If your tease lands after the cut, move it up.
05
Audit the extras
Your headline and image are part of the hook too.
Does your headline make you worth listening to?
Pull them in + tease the rest = a hook worth clicking
